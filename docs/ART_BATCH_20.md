# Remaining creature art batch: 20 catalog prints

This manifest reserves the missing launch-catalog presentation assets. The four
existing living sheets and raw prints (Cinderpaw, Aquafin, Voltwig, Mossmunk)
are approved inputs and must remain untouched. Primary art delivery should add
only the paths below: a 1536x1024 transparent living 4x2 sheet and one
transparent dormant raw-print PNG per row.

Living grid contract: 4 columns by 2 rows, each 384x512 cell. Cells 0-3 are
idle; 4 is wind-up; 5 strike; 6 recovery; 7 hit. Raw prints are the separate
fresh-off-the-build-plate forms used by the Arcane Forge before awakening.
