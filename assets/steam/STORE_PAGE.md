# Steam Store Page — PrintForge Legends

## Short Description (max ~30 words)

Collect magical 3D-printed creatures, battle AI trainers, and grow your Forge in this unique creature collector with educational mini-games built right in.

## About This Game

Welcome to **Forgeholm** — a futuristic magical workshop where arcane energy powers giant 3D printers and every creature you collect is a real print brought to life.

In **PrintForge Legends**, you are an apprentice Forge Master building a team of printed creatures, battling trainers across the city, and expanding your workshop one blueprint at a time.

**Collect & Print** — Buy blueprint instructions at the Filament Market, spend Forge Coins to print new creatures, and fill your Print Yard with a living collection. Each creature has elemental types, stats, and abilities that matter in battle.

**Turn-Based Battles** — Fight on the Proving Grounds with a mix of creature abilities, filament cards, and a Forge Bar that builds power every turn. Outsmart AI trainers, earn XP and coins, and occasionally score rare blueprint drops.

**The Academy** — Five educational quest lines teach math through the world of the forge: addition, multiplication, division, percentages, and order of operations. Kids learn; adults still get a full battle game underneath.

**Gear & Growth** — Equip armor, weapons, and accessories in the Gear Locker. Level up your Spool, track every creature in the Forgedex, and work toward becoming the ultimate printer in Forgeholm.

No internet required. One save file. Family-friendly. Built for Steam.
