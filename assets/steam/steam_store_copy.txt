<h2>Welcome to Forgeholm</h2>

In a world where ancient arcane energy powers enormous 3D printing machines,
you are a <strong>Forge Apprentice</strong> — a collector, a builder, and a
battler. PrintForge Legends is a creature-collecting battle game unlike any
other, set in a futuristic magical workshop where every creature you own is
one you built yourself.

<h2>Collect. Print. Battle.</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Discover blueprints</strong> in the rotating Filament Market, earn
them through educational mini-games, or find them as rare battle drops.</li>
<li><strong>Print your creatures</strong> using your in-game Forge — the more
complex the design, the more powerful the Print.</li>
<li><strong>Equip armor, weapons, and accessories</strong> to customize your
team's stats and abilities before battle.</li>
<li><strong>Battle AI trainers</strong> in turn-based combat — from the
beginner-friendly Tutorial Trainer to the fearsome Forge Master.</li>
</ul>

<h2>The Bend Score System</h2>

Every creature's power is determined by its <strong>Bend Score</strong> — the
number of geometric bends in its 3D model. Simple creatures are fast and cheap
to print. Complex legendary creatures with intricate designs have massive power.
Bigger really does mean stronger.

<h2>8 Elements. Deep Strategy.</h2>

Fire beats Nature. Water beats Fire. Lightning beats Water. Combine creature
types, abilities, and equipment to build a team with real strategic depth. Every
battle rewards smart play over grinding.

<h2>Learn While You Play</h2>

The Academy offers math mini-games from basic addition all the way to
pre-algebra. Complete quests to earn <strong>Forge Coins</strong> — the
currency you need to buy blueprint instructions and print new creatures. The
better you do, the more you earn.

<h2>Your Workshop, Your World</h2>

Every player has a personal Workshop hub — a living space where your printed
creatures roam free in the Print Yard, your trophy wall displays your victories,
and your Filament Spool grows larger and more elaborate as you level up.

<h2>The Forgedex</h2>

Discover, collect, and track all 15 launch creatures in your Forgedex. Spot a
creature in battle? It appears as a silhouette — a mystery waiting to be
unlocked. The full roster is never revealed. Some prints are so rare that almost
nobody has seen them.

<h2>Features</h2>

<ul>
<li>15 unique creatures across 5 rarity tiers at launch</li>
<li>Full equipment system — armor, weapons, and accessories</li>
<li>5 AI trainers with escalating difficulty</li>
<li>4 educational math quest types</li>
<li>8-element type chart with strategic matchups</li>
<li>Local save — no internet required, no account needed</li>
<li>Steam Cloud Save support</li>
<li>Steam achievements</li>
</ul>
